Amader Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

This policy explains how [YOUR LEGAL NAME or AMADER LLC] ("Amader") handles consumer health data, as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws in other states such as Nevada. Amader is not a medical app, but some of what families share about their meals could be treated as health information under these laws, so we treat it that way.

Consumer health data we collect, and why

Category Examples Why we collect it
Meals and eating habits Meal photos, when a meal was eaten, which starch it had (for example rice or roti) and how much, whether you started with vegetables To share meals with your family table and show weekly recaps
Eating goals The small step you picked (for example "at most 2½ handfuls at a meal"), which step you are on and since when To show your path to you and your family
App use Days the app was opened, photo guesses made and changed, help opened To learn what works during the pilot. Never shown to your family
Information worked out from meals Dish name, rough split of the plate, portion guess, weekly patterns To fill in the meal and recap for you, only if you turned on photo reading
A summary you can bring to a checkup A list of your own recent meals and portions, made on your phone So you can show your meals to your own doctor if you choose
Learning examples The app's guess and the amount you saved for a meal To improve food recognition, only if you turned on "Help Amader learn our food"

We do not collect health readings, diagnoses, medicines, or biometric data.

If a family table goes unused for 12 months, it is deleted. Learning examples that people chose to share are then kept without any name, family, code or sign-in id, and with only the month, so they are no longer linked to anyone. Hand measuring happens on your phone and the hand is never saved or sent to us.

Where it comes from

Directly from you, through the app. Some of it (the dish name and plate split) is worked out from your photo by the app and our AI service provider, only with your consent.

Who we share it with

Who What Why
The members of your family table, who you invite with your family code Your meals, photos, portions, dish names, hearts and your path This is what the app is for
Supabase (our database and storage provider) All of the above To store it and run the app for us
Anthropic (our AI service provider) Meal photos and the name of your goal food To read the photo, only after you said yes
Expo, then Apple or Google (notification delivery) A first name and the meal time, never what was eaten To deliver family notifications, only if you turned them on

We do not have affiliates. We do not sell consumer health data, and we never will without the signed authorization the law requires. We do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

You have the right to: - confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and see it; - get a list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with, and how to contact them; - withdraw your consent; - have it deleted, including copies held by the companies we share it with.

How to do this: - In the app: Me, "Delete my meals and leave" deletes everything. Me, Photos turns off photo reading and learning examples. "Remove" under any meal deletes that meal. - By email: [CONTACT EMAIL]. We will answer within 45 days. If we need more time we will tell you why, and we may take up to 45 more days.

If we turn down your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. We will answer the appeal within 45 days. If you are not satisfied you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

Consent

We ask for your consent before we collect consumer health data, when you set up or join a family table. We ask separately before any photo is read by our AI service provider, and before any learning example is kept. You can withdraw consent at any time as described above.
